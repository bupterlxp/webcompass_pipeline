Scales

Major Scale

The major scale follows the pattern: W-W-H-W-W-W-H

C D E F G A B

Minor Scale

The natural minor scale follows the pattern: W-H-W-W-H-W-W

A B C D E F G

Chromatic Scale

All 12 notes in an octave, each a half step apart.

C C# D D# E F F# G G# A A# B

Chords

Major Chords

Constructed from root, major third, and perfect fifth.

C Major

C - E - G

G Major

G - B - D

D Major

D - F# - A

Minor Chords

Constructed from root, minor third, and perfect fifth.

A Minor

A - C - E

E Minor

E - G - B

F Minor

F - Ab - C

7th Chords

Constructed from root, third, fifth, and seventh.

C7

C - E - G - Bb

G7

G - B - D - F

D7

D - F# - A - C

About Music Theory Learning

This website is designed to help musicians of all levels learn and practice music theory concepts. From basic scales and chords to practical progression exercises, this platform provides interactive learning with audio examples to reinforce understanding.

Features include:

  • Interactive scale and chord lessons
  • Audio examples for each concept
  • Practice exercises with instant feedback
  • Progression building with correctness checking

Whether you're a beginner learning your first chord or an advanced musician looking to deepen your understanding, this site offers valuable resources to enhance your musical knowledge.
