The quiet joy of a solo morning hike

By Luna · March 14, 2025 · 6 min read

There is a particular kind of silence that only exists before the world wakes up. It settles over the forest like a blanket, soft and unhurried. I found it this morning on a trail I've walked a hundred times, but today it felt like the first.

Three hours, one trail, no destination. Just the sound of my boots on pine needles, the occasional bird call, and the slow unfurling of light across the canopy. I stopped counting steps somewhere around the third hour.

"The mountains are calling, and I must go." — John Muir

I carried nothing but a water bottle and a notebook. I wrote three sentences. I took two photos. I let the silence do the rest.

If you've never done a solo morning hike, I urge you. The world is too loud for its own good, and the forest remembers how to be quiet.
