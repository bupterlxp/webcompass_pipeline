Background
Born into the declining Shen Clan of the Eastern Provinces, Yueyan was considered a cripple with blocked meridians until the age of sixteen. During a near-death encounter in the Abyssal Flame Caverns, the dormant Yan-Huang bloodline within her awakened violently, burning away her impurities and reforging her entire body. In a single night, she leapt from the Qi Condensation realm to Core Formation — a feat unheard of in ten thousand years. Now pursued by ancient factions who seek to either control or extinguish the flame of the Yan-Huang, she walks a path between glory and annihilation, carrying the weight of an imperial legacy on her young shoulders.
