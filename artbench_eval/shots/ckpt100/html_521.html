Theory

Foundations of Western music.

Scales

A scale is a sequence of notes ordered by pitch. The most common is the major scale, built from whole and half steps: W-W-H-W-W-W-H.

Chords

Triads built on three notes. Major = root, major 3rd, perfect 5th. Minor = root, minor 3rd, perfect 5th.

Intervals

The distance between two notes. A major third is four semitones (e.g., C to E). A perfect fifth is seven semitones (e.g., C to G).
