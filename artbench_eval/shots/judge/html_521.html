Musical Scales

A scale is any set of musical notes ordered by fundamental frequency or pitch. A scale ordered by increasing pitch is an ascending scale, and a scale ordered by decreasing pitch is a descending scale.

The Major Scale

The major scale is one of the most commonly used musical scales, especially in Western music. It is made up of seven notes. The pattern of whole and half steps is: Whole, Whole, Half, Whole, Whole, Whole, Half.

The Minor Scale (Natural)

The natural minor scale sounds "sadder" or more serious compared to the major scale. Its step pattern is: Whole, Half, Whole, Whole, Half, Whole, Whole.

Musical Chords

A chord, in music, is any harmonic set of pitches consisting of multiple notes (usually three or more) that are heard as if sounding simultaneously.

Major Triads

A major triad consists of a root note, a major third (4 semitones above the root), and a perfect fifth (7 semitones above the root). It generally has a "happy" or "resolved" sound.

Minor Triads

A minor triad consists of a root note, a minor third (3 semitones above the root), and a perfect fifth (7 semitones above the root). It generally has a "melancholy" sound.
